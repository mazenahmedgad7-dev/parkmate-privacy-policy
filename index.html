ParkMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Welcome to ParkMate. Your privacy is important to us.

1. Location Information

ParkMate may request access to your device's GPS location to identify your current location and provide location-based parking information.

You can control location permissions through your device settings. Location information may be processed by third-party map or location providers if these services are used by the app.

2. Personal Information

ParkMate does not require users to create accounts. The app does not provide online parking reservations or online payment features.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information processed by third-party services is subject to their own privacy policies.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information associated with the app.

5. Children's Privacy

ParkMate is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
